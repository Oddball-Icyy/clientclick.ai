FAQ

Questions local business owners actually ask us.

What kinds of businesses do you work with? +
We work with any local or home service business — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, cleaning, painting, flooring, general contractors, and more. If you sell a service to homeowners or local property owners, our system is built for you.
How quickly will I see real results? +
Most clients see their first booked appointments within 7–14 days of launch. Local service intent is high — when someone's AC breaks or a pipe bursts, they need someone today. Our system is built to be the first responder every time.
What does "exclusive territory" mean exactly? +
Once you become a client, we won't onboard any direct competitor targeting the same zip codes. We can't run conflicting campaigns for businesses competing for the same customers — it would undermine your results. Your territory is locked when you sign.
Do I need to be tech-savvy? +
Not at all. Our Done-For-You and Done-With-You tiers require zero technical involvement from you. We build, configure, and manage everything. You just need to show up for the jobs we book into your calendar.
What's the guarantee? +
If we don't hit the performance targets we agreed on in your first 30 days, we work for free until we do — or we issue a refund. We define the targets together upfront so there's no ambiguity. We've never had to invoke this, but it exists to give you zero-risk confidence.
How does the AI lead qualification work? +
When a lead comes in through any channel — ad, website, missed call, or DM — our AI responds within 60 seconds. It asks qualifying questions, checks for budget and timeline fit, answers common objections, and either books the appointment automatically or routes the hot lead to you for a personal call.
What ad platforms do you use? +
Primarily Meta (Facebook + Instagram) and Google Search, including Local Service Ads for eligible industries. We select the right mix based on your trade, service area, and average job value. Some accounts also benefit from TikTok or YouTube for brand awareness campaigns.
Can I start small and upgrade later? +
Absolutely. Many clients start with Tier 01 or Tier 02 to get comfortable with the system, then upgrade once they see results compounding. Each tier is designed to stack on top of the previous one — your investment grows more powerful as you scale up.
